How to record

Who he is

A normal guy sitting at the top of the cave with a walkie-talkie. He's climbed all of this himself, more than once. Calm, dry, a little tired. He's not a narrator. Think of being on the phone with a friend who's halfway up a cliff.

How to sound

  • Talk, don't perform. Say it the way you'd actually say it to a friend.
  • Small smiles are good. Big emotion isn't. The sad lines get quieter and simpler, not more dramatic.
  • Don't put on a radio voice. The game adds the walkie-talkie sound itself (except in the ending).
  • Reactions repeat a lot, so keep those light and quick.
  • Read the "How" note under each line before you hit record.

Setup

  • Quietest room you've got. A closet full of clothes makes a great booth.
  • Use the phone's own mic. Take your AirPods out: their mic sounds like a phone call.
  • Phone about a hand's width from your mouth, a little off to the side so P's and B's don't pop.
  • Same spot and distance for every line, so they all match.
  • Tap Record, wait half a second, then talk. Leave a beat at the end before you tap Stop. The app cuts the gaps off for you.

iPhone: turn on Voice Isolation

  1. Record any line once (that turns the mic on).
  2. While it's recording, swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
  3. Tap the Safari controls at the top, then under Mic Mode pick Voice Isolation.
  4. It stays on for Safari after that. Redo the first line once it's on.

If Mic Mode isn't there, your iPhone doesn't offer it for Safari. The cleanup below still runs.

Settings

When you're done

Tap Export and download the zip, or share it to Google Drive or iCloud Drive. On your PC, put Voice.zip in Downloads\Cavediving, right-click it and pick Extract All. That makes the Voice folder. Then tell Claude "import voices". Every file is already named after its line, like wake_1.wav.

Takes are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back later. iPhone can clear a site's data if you don't open it for a while, so export a backup zip now and then. You can load a zip back in from the Export screen. On a computer, Space starts and stops recording, the arrow keys change lines, P plays the take and N jumps to the next unrecorded line.

Export

Each file is named after its line (wake_1.wav, setback_3.wav ...). On your PC, put Voice.zip in Downloads\Cavediving, right-click it and pick Extract All. That makes the Voice folder. Then tell Claude "import voices". A half-done zip is fine too: lines without a recording just show as text in the game.

Load takes

Put back a zip you exported before, or .wav files named like wake_1.wav. Handy on a new phone, or if Safari cleared its data.
